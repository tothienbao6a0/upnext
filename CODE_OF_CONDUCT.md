# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent to each other. Assume the other person is trying to do good work with
incomplete information, because usually they are.

## What that means here

Technical disagreement is welcome and is most of what a project like this is
for. Argue about the design as hard as you like — argue about capability
semantics, about whether the queue belongs here, about whether a decision was
wrong. Criticise the code, the API and the reasoning freely.

What is not welcome is aimed at people rather than work: harassment, personal
or demeaning remarks, sustained disruption, unwelcome attention, or publishing
someone's private information. Nor is dismissing someone on the basis of who
they are — experience level, background, identity, or how well they write
English.

Reviews should be about whether the change is right, and should say why. "This
races with X" is useful. "This is obviously wrong" is not.

## Enforcement

Report anything that needs attention to the maintainers, privately, at
tothienbao6a0@gmail.com. Reports are handled confidentially.

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove contributions that do not fit this
document, and may block accounts for repeated or serious behaviour. Decisions
will be explained to the person concerned wherever it is reasonable to do so.

## Scope

This applies in the repository — issues, pull requests, code, commits and
reviews — and anywhere someone is representing the project.
